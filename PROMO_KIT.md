# Plug Run promo kit

Status: branded covers and copy are ready for review. Gameplay screenshots and trailers must be captured from actual current gameplay; this package does not substitute illustrations or old-map replays for real play.

## Finished assets

- `promo/plug-run-cover-1200x630.svg` — landscape social/link cover.
- `promo/plug-run-poster-1080x1920.svg` — vertical poster/story cover.
- `PROMO_COPY.md` — short and long launch copy.

These vectors reuse the game's current wordmark, crew sigils, and palette. They are editorial graphics, not screenshots. Export PNGs at native dimensions for platforms that do not accept SVG.

## Real gameplay deliverables to capture

Save five unaltered browser captures in `promo/screenshots/`:

1. `01-choose-your-crew.png` — The Window / crew selection, readable on a phone.
2. `02-stash-and-plug.png` — runner, real stash, Plug, and car visible in a live house.
3. `03-narrow-exit.png` — a genuine near-miss on the way to the car, captured from a real run.
4. `04-character-moment.png` — one legible, spoiler-light in-game contact exchange.
5. `05-block-rivals.png` — the real Rivals UI or an actual in-game race, without claiming a live human opponent.

Use current builds and maps, hide personal account data, and keep the genuine HUD. Do not fabricate gameplay, splice a replay onto a different map, or change physics for a shot. If a narrow escape cannot be captured honestly, use a normal extraction instead.

## Trailer deliverables

- Vertical: 1080×1920 H.264 MP4, 20–30 seconds, 30 fps.
- Landscape: 1920×1080 H.264 MP4, 45–60 seconds, 30 fps.
- Keep uncut source clips and an edit project or reproducible edit commands.
- End with the live site: `plug-run.io`.
- Use only audio cleared for promotional distribution; gameplay use does not automatically establish trailer music rights.

See `CLAUDE_TRAILER_CAPTURE_PROMPT.md` for the capture and edit brief. Do not publish the videos or screenshots until someone has watched the final exports on a phone and desktop.
