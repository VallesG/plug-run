# Plug Run — beat brief

Creative target: **100 BPM, D minor**. This is a suggested shared palette, not a detected key/tempo of our existing tracks. Other keys are welcome; 92–108 BPM or a double-time feel works too.

Dark, playful street energy for a fast 2D arcade game: punchy hip-hop, dusty breaks, restrained synths, garage/shop textures, or late-night club bounce. Make it fun rather than cinematic doom. Each beat should have its own personality without requiring a faction assignment.

- Supply seamless **16- or 32-bar loops**, plus an optional longer arrangement. No long intro, silence, or huge end crash. Test repeating the loop several times.
- Keep melody and bass memorable but leave space for pickups, powers, shots, engines, and reading dialogue. Avoid constant vocals, alarms, sirens, or gunshot-like percussion that could sound like gameplay warnings.
- Send a stereo **24-bit / 48 kHz WAV master**, with clean headroom and no clipping. Also send aligned drums, bass, melody, and atmosphere stems starting at the same point. Keep masters outside the game's public/deploy folder.
- Include BPM, key, bar count, title, producer credit, and confirmation that samples are cleared for game use. We encode the runtime MP3/OGG versions.
- Optional: a short 1–2 second victory flourish matching the beat, and a slightly bigger city-finish version. Avoid voice tags; the crew dialogue should remain readable.

In game, tracks shuffle without immediate repeats at new blocks and Rivals races, not at every door or death. Music drops behind character panels; existing street ambience and gameplay effects stay.

Current shuffle pool: main_beat, plug_beat2, learn_beat. The separate unused plug_beat is not included without review. New completion/contact cues are original lightweight Web Audio earcons, not downloaded samples.
