Plug Run Privacy Policy

Last updated September 24, 2026 · plugrun.io and the @PlugRunBot Telegram Mini App

Plug Run is a free browser game. This page explains what information the game collects when you play at plugrun.io or inside Telegram, what it is used for, and how to have it deleted. We do not sell your information and we do not show ads.

What we collect

  • Your game progress: campaign, crew, contacts, Block Rivals progress, settings, STASH and stats. These are stored in your browser. In Telegram they are also backed up to Telegram's CloudStorage for our bot, so they follow you to your other devices.
  • A player identity: a random player ID, a display name, a login token and a recovery code, stored on our server so leaderboards and shared runs know which player they belong to.
  • From Telegram, when you open Plug Run there: Telegram passes the game your Telegram user ID, first name, username and language. We check Telegram's signature, then keep only your Telegram user ID (to link it to your player identity) and use your first name as your display name. We do not store your username, phone number or profile photo.
  • Leaderboard results: scores and times with your display name.
  • Shared Block Rivals runs: if "Share my run" is on (it is by default, and can be switched off before each race), a recording of your race and your display name are sent to us. After review they may appear to other players as a rival to race. The stored run is labelled by your display name and a key derived from your player ID, not the ID itself.
  • Daily Race runs: your official Daily Race run is sent to us with its recording, so it can be checked and ranked. Your time and display name appear on that day's leaderboard, and the recording is kept so prize results can be reviewed.
  • Prize winners: if you win a Daily Race prize and claim it, we keep the TON wallet address you connect and a record of the payment we send to it. Payments on the TON blockchain are public.
  • Challenges: when you challenge a friend, we store the race (course, rival, your time) with your display name for 30 days, and count how many people opened, played and beat it.
  • Analytics: we use Google Analytics to see how the game is played. It receives gameplay events (such as a house started or a race finished), the kind of device and browser, whether you are playing on the web or in Telegram, and an approximate location derived from your IP address, which is anonymized. In Telegram the game gives Google Analytics a random ID it keeps for this purpose. We never send Google Analytics your player ID, Telegram ID, name or recovery code.
  • Server logs: our hosting provider records standard request logs, including IP addresses, to run and protect the site.

Where it is kept

The site and its server functions run on Netlify. Player identities, leaderboards and challenges are stored in Upstash (Redis). Shared runs are stored in Netlify Blobs. Analytics is processed by Google. Telegram stores what you back up to its CloudStorage and handles your Telegram account under its own terms and privacy policy.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't show ads or share your information with advertisers.
  • We don't message you on Telegram except to reply when you message @PlugRunBot, and to tell you when you win a Daily Race prize and when it has been paid.
  • We don't take payments, and we never ask for your wallet's seed phrase or keys.

What other players can see

Your display name (in Telegram, your first name) appears on leaderboards, on shared runs other players race against, and on challenge cards you send.

How long we keep it

Challenges expire after 30 days. Player identities, leaderboard results, shared runs, Daily Race recordings and prize records are kept while the game runs, unless you ask us to delete them. Progress stored in your browser stays until you clear it.

Deleting your information

Email me@gilvalles.com with your display name (and, if you play in Telegram, your Telegram username so we can find the linked identity), and we will delete your player identity, leaderboard entries, shared runs, Daily Race recordings and challenges (a record of a prize payment already sent is kept for our accounts). You can clear the progress on your device by clearing the site's data in your browser. In Telegram, stopping and deleting the chat with @PlugRunBot removes its access to your account.

Children

Plug Run is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
