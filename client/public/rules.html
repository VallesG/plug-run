Daily Race Prize Rules

Last updated September 24, 2026 · Plug Run in Telegram (@PlugRunBot)

On prize days, the fastest official Daily Race time from a Telegram player wins the prize shown in the game. Entry is free: Plug Run has nothing to buy, and nothing you could pay for would improve your time.

Who can win

  • Players racing in Plug Run's Telegram Mini App (@PlugRunBot), signed in with their Telegram account. Runs played only on the web are ranked but cannot win a prize.
  • You must be 18 or older, or the age of majority where you live if that is higher.
  • One Telegram account per person.
  • Not open where skill contests with prizes, or prizes paid in cryptocurrency, are not allowed; to anyone on a U.S. government sanctions list; or to residents of countries and regions under comprehensive U.S. sanctions.

How the race works

  • Each Daily Race runs from midnight to midnight UTC. Everyone races the same course, stash layout and rival.
  • Your official run is your first race of the day to reach GO. If you quit, close the game or lose your connection, that run still counts as your official run.
  • Clear all seven houses to set a time. Every official run is checked from its recording; a run that fails the checks is not ranked. Races after your official run are for fun and never replace it.

The prize

  • A prize day is announced in the game, with its amount (for example, $5 in TON). One prize per prize day.
  • The prize is paid in Toncoin (TON) worth the stated U.S. dollar amount at the time we send it. We pay the network fee.
  • The winner is the fastest verified official time from an eligible Telegram player. If two times are equal, the run that reached the server first wins.

Winning and claiming

  • The winner is decided at 00:30 UTC after the day ends. We tell the winner in the game, and in Telegram if they have allowed @PlugRunBot to message them.
  • To claim, open Plug Run in Telegram and connect a TON wallet through TON Connect within 7 days. An unclaimed prize is forfeited.
  • We send prizes within 7 days of a claim, to the wallet you connected.

We will never ask for your seed phrase, private keys or password, or ask you to send money or crypto to receive a prize. Anyone who does is not Plug Run.

Fair play

We review the winning run before paying. We may disqualify a run that uses cheats, bots, modified game clients, multiple accounts or exploits, and pass the prize to the next eligible player. Our decisions on results are final.

Taxes

Winners are responsible for any taxes on their prize. The value of TON changes; the amount of TON is set when the prize is sent.

Changes

We may change, pause or end prizes, or these rules, at any time. A change never affects a prize day already underway: the prize shown for a day stands for that day.

Privacy

A winner's TON wallet address and the payment are recorded, and payments on the TON blockchain are public. See the privacy policy.

Not affiliated

This prize is not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Telegram or the TON Foundation.
