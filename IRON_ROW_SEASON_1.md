2. Three Season 1 Narrative Pitches
Option A: Somebody’s Keeping Count (Recommended)

Opening Incident: In Block 1, runners discover that stashes are not sitting loose in floorboards; they are organized, tagged with brass inventory chits, and marked with running tally notches. Someone is conducting an active audit of the city's stash houses.

Crew Stakes:

Crossline: Switch realizes a third party is mapping their drop points and courier frequencies. If this network falls into outside hands, their routing infrastructure dies.

Iron Row: Rook discovers the brass tags carry serial numbers identical to an old municipal impound inventory. Brick fears an organized precinct or private security racket is preparing asset seizures on their garage.

Afterlight: Vee and Sol hear rumors that a high-roller syndicate is scouting the top crew for an exclusive closed-door circuit. Sol wants to run faster to top the invisible score; Vee wants to find the scout to hijack their spotlight.

Midpoint Revelation (Block 5): The "Counter" is not the law or an outside syndicate. An anonymous broker has been taking stashes from all three crews, laundering the proceeds through Auntie Ro’s supply chain, and using the runs to test which crew is ruthless enough to take over the city core. Auntie Ro denies complicity, but she knew the counting was happening and stayed silent to protect her store.

Block 10 Payoff: The final drop house reveals the auditor's identity and ledger. Crossline secures the master tracking frequency to shield their runners; Iron Row seizes the equipment deeds to fortify their shop; Afterlight steals the spotlight by throwing a massive street takeover at the auditor's central hub. The auditor flees into City 2, leaving the district claimed by the player's crew, but with the broader city network wide open.

Option B: The Paper Condemnations

Opening Incident: Bright orange condemnation notices appear on stash houses across Block 1. Demolition crews are slated to level houses across ten blocks, compressing the territory and forcing the Plugs into tighter, deadlier perimeters.

Crew Stakes:

Crossline: Switch must retrieve offline server bricks and route repeaters hidden in condemned walls before bulldozers tear them down.

Iron Row: Brick needs heavy architectural salvage—industrial copper, load-bearing I-beams, and old generators—to reinforce their underground garage before redevelopment seals them out.

Afterlight: Vee vows to turn the condemned strip into a massive rolling art installation and night-race run before the blocks fall, using the chaos to humiliate the city authorities.

Midpoint Revelation (Block 5): The condemnation orders are forged. A predatory commercial developer is using fake notices and private enforcers (the Plugs) to panic residents, flush out the stash runners, and buy up ten blocks of urban land at salvage rates.

Block 10 Payoff: The runner clears House 150 right as wrecking balls idle at the curb. The crew secures the forged master deed from the site office. Crossline broadcasts the fraud across city comms; Iron Row physically bar-welds the developer's machinery; Afterlight documents the whole farce on building-sized murals. The immediate neighborhood is saved, but the developer retreats to the downtown skyline (City 2).

Option C: The Clean Slate

Opening Incident: A retired legendary runner, "Ghost" Marcus, disappears from the district, leaving behind a string of unresolved debts and hidden personal safe boxes across ten blocks.

Crew Stakes:

Crossline: Marcus had the master floorplans for the district's blind spots. Switch wants those tactical maps to build an untouchable courier web.

Iron Row: Marcus owned the deed and machine tooling inside Brick’s garage. Brick and Rook must recover Marcus’s physical promissory notes before outside repo men strip their bays.

Afterlight: Marcus held the legendary sprint records across every major avenue. Sol is obsessed with beating Marcus's ghost splits, while Vee wants Marcus's vintage getaway car as Afterlight’s flagship centerpiece.

Midpoint Revelation (Block 5): Marcus did not retire—he was squeezed out by Auntie Ro after attempting to sell the neighborhood's safe-house coordinates to outside syndicates. The safe boxes runners have been raiding are Marcus's contingency stash, meant to pay off the Plugs to hunt down the three crews.

Block 10 Payoff: The runner raids Marcus’s primary bunker at House 150. With Marcus neutralized, Crossline owns the new map, Iron Row secures the permanent garage deed, and Afterlight claims the legendary runner's custom ride. Marcus flees to the industrial harbor (City 2) to rally his outside backers.
