# Plug Run — Season One (updated script)

Source: "Plug Run - Season One Updated" (revised character + systems pass). The game plays this
text from `client/src/logic/{crossline,ironRow,afterlight}Season.js`; each DOOR beat plays before
that house, DOOR 15 is the finish after extraction, and "Duskport" follows the block's city.
House pass on top of the writer's draft: punched-up jokes (puns, Gen Z voice for Mags, Rook
and Sol; Switch and Brick stay deadpan), no map descriptions (the crews never narrate a layout
the player can see is not there), and "N clear / N left" counts that match the door.

**Three crews, one season.** 30 chapters - 10 per crew - 15 doors per block - set in Duskport - ends pointed at Copper Bay

Revision goal: lived-in relationships, distinct crew humor, surprise, clean gameplay callouts, and late-season escalation toward Copper Bay without flattening the characters into tutorial voices.

Continuity: Brick is a woman. Every reference in this revision treats her accordingly.

## Voice bible

Switch: Dry, controlled, old-school. Jokes by refusing to joke. Protective without admitting it.

Mags: Fast, technical, competitive, teasing. Uses humor when she is nervous; hates being caught sincere.

Brick: Woman. Garage foreman energy: terse, practical, scary calm, accidentally maternal. Says less than everyone else and usually wins the exchange.

Rook: Talkative improviser. Jokes to fill silence, notices people more than he pretends, moral streak under the hustle.

Vee: Art-brained, composed, image-conscious, sharp. Turns insecurity into taste and control.

Sol: Excitable hype man with timer brain. Funny on purpose until the subject gets personal, then unexpectedly gentle.

Runner arc: early chapters call the player “runner” and give clean instructions. Mid-season, the crews tease and recognize habits. By chapter ten, the runner is spoken to like a known teammate rather than replaceable labor.

Copper Bay arc: the final stretch increasingly points to bigger stashes paired with harder, multi-Plug houses. The name “the Connect” surfaces as a title attached to a powerful, unseen supplier behind Copper Bay’s Plug economy, without revealing who they are yet.

## How to read the beats

DOOR NUMBERS: Each block is still fifteen houses. Door 9 remains the fixed mission-item pickup. Other dialogue beats move around so the season does not feel mechanically identical.

REACTIVE LINES: Each performance-dependent debrief has blank conditional opening slots for: flawless, comeback, zero deaths, no powers used, phase used, dash used, decoy used, and a neutral fallback. One slot can later receive crew-specific spoken praise; the fixed dialogue that follows stays unchanged.

PERSONAL ITEMS: Two per crew remain, but their emotional setup leaks into earlier chapters and their consequences can recur later.

RIVAL FRICTION: One per crew remains. The pass leaves room for manipulation, misunderstandings, and humor instead of making every inter-crew clue automatically true.

COPPER BAY / THE CONNECT: Late blocks foreshadow the tradeoff: bigger stashes, harder multi-Plug houses. “The Connect” is treated as a title for the unseen person who supplies Copper Bay’s Plugs; identity, reach, and motive remain unresolved.

BLOCK COMPLETE: Closers advance relationship or mystery, not just the cumulative stash counter.
